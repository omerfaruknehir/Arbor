Google ML Kit Text Recognition

Arbor uses the on-device Google ML Kit Text Recognition Android library.
Use of the library and its binary components is governed by Google's ML Kit
terms and the terms distributed with the Android artifact.

Official terms:

Product documentation:

does not relicense the ML Kit binary distributed through Google's Maven
