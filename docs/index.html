Open-source Android AI client

A BYOK AI chat app for Android

Xylune (pronounced “Zy-loon”) is a native, local-first Android chat client for people who want to bring their own API keys, provider accounts, or local model servers instead of being locked into one AI service.

Bring your own key

Use your own AI providers from one Android app

Connect OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, xAI, ChatGPT, OpenAI-compatible endpoints, or a compatible local model server. Xylune sends requests directly from your Android device to the provider or server you select, without a Xylune relay backend.

  • BYOK multi-provider chat: use your own API access and switch between supported providers and models.
  • Local-first storage: encrypted credentials, a local SQLCipher chat database, app-private files, and portable backups.
  • Native Android tools: streaming chat, search, files, Markdown, LaTeX, Python, optional Linux tools, and programmable widgets.

What does BYOK mean?

BYOK means bring your own key. Instead of buying AI access from Xylune, you connect credentials for a provider you already use. The provider remains responsible for its own model access and billing, while Xylune provides the Android chat interface and local workspace.

shield

Cloud backups move directly between the user’s device and the destination they select. Xylune does not operate a central backup service.
