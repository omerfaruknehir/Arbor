Android BYOK AI client

One native app for your AI providers

Bring your own API keys, a supported ChatGPT account, or a compatible local model server. Xylune sends requests directly from your Android device to the service you select, without a Xylune relay backend.

  • Multi-provider chat: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, xAI, ChatGPT, and OpenAI-compatible endpoints.
  • Local-first storage: encrypted credentials, a local SQLCipher chat database, app-private files, and portable backups.
  • Native Android tools: streaming chat, search, files, Markdown, LaTeX, Python, optional Linux tools, and programmable widgets.
shield

Cloud backups move directly between the user’s device and the destination they select. Xylune does not operate a central backup service.
